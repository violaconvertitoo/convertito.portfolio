Where Are the Rural Voices? Mapping Rural Representation in EU Parliaments

Interactive R Shiny map of rural-born parliamentarians across six European countries, 1999–2019

R Shinysf / leafletInteractive mappingPolitical representation

Research question and approach

Are people born in rural areas becoming less visible in national parliaments? This descriptive project maps the share of rural-born parliamentarians in Germany, France, Spain, Ireland, the Czech Republic and Austria between 1999 and 2019, using parliamentarians’ birthplace classifications.

Explore the interactive map

Select a country to compare rural, town/suburban and city birthplaces by political party; hover over countries to view the rural-born share; and use the year slider to explore changes over time.

The Shiny application is hosted externally and may take a moment to wake up. If embedding is unavailable, open it directly.

Open full interactive R Shiny map ↗

Descriptive findings and interpretation

Ireland has the highest rural-born share among the six countries studied: approximately 50% in 1999 and 37.69% in 2019. In the other countries, the observed shares were generally lower, with declines over the period.

Important limitation: birthplace is not the same as current residence or constituency. The map is descriptive; it cannot establish whether rural-born people have lower probabilities of entering parliament or whether birthplace directly influences policy priorities. It raises questions about geographic background, political recruitment and rural representation for further research.
