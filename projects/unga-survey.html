UNGA AMR Commitments — Global Stakeholder Survey

Stakeholder-reported implementation, contributions, barriers and priorities under the 2024 UN Political Declaration on AMR

KoboToolboxSurvey designOne HealthPolicy monitoring

Purpose and objectives

The AMR Multi-Stakeholder Partnership Platform survey captures stakeholders’ direct experiences of implementing the 2024 United Nations General Assembly Political Declaration on antimicrobial resistance. It asks where implementation stands, what enables or slows delivery, what stakeholders contribute, and where collective action should accelerate toward 2029.

Its results are stakeholder-reported evidence and perspectives, complementing official monitoring and reporting rather than evaluating country performance.

Survey design and implementation

I translated the policy commitments into a structured KoboToolbox/XLSForm questionnaire spanning One Health sectors, implementation maturity, barriers, enabling conditions, contributions and priorities. The instrument combines conditional questions, standardized response choices and evidence prompts. Respondents are instructed to assess only areas they know and to select Unable to assess rather than guess.
