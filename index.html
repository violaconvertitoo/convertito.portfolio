About

I hold a Master of International Affairs from the Hertie School, specializing in quantitative methods, and a BA in Political Science and International Relations from Sapienza University of Rome. My experience combines quantitative research, impact evaluation and evidence-based policy work. At the International Security and Development Center (ISDC), I contributed to an RCT-based impact evaluation in Syria, working with large-scale household survey data and food security indicators. At FAO, I have supported research and policy initiatives through survey instrument development, statistical analysis, stakeholder engagement and the translation of evidence into practical tools and policy-relevant outputs.
